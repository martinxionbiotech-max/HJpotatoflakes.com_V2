# Application Development Records

Our application development records document real-world R&D trials conducted by Hongji Agriculture's technical team, demonstrating how potato flakes and potato powder perform across diverse food manufacturing applications. Each record includes the formulation approach, processing parameters, analytical results, and sensory evaluation — providing actionable guidance for product developers and R&D professionals evaluating potato ingredients for their specific applications.

## Application Trial Portfolio

| Application | Key Challenge | Solution | Result |
|:-----------|:-------------|:--------|:------:|
| Gluten-Free Bread | Poor crumb structure, dryness | 30% potato flour + hydrocolloid blend | 85% wheat-like crumb softness |
| Cream Soup Viscosity | Inconsistent thickness | 6% potato powder + shear optimization | Stable viscosity, 12-month shelf life |
| Extruded Snacks | Density control, expansion ratio | 25% potato flakes + moisture profiling | Consistent bulk density ±5% |
| Pet Food Palatability | Low acceptance rates | 18% flakes + palatant optimization | 92% acceptance (vs 78% control) |
| Meat Binder WHC | Cooking loss, texture variation | 10% flakes with brine optimization | 15% yield improvement, consistent texture |
