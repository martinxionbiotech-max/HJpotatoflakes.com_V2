# Food Fraud Vulnerability Assessment Record

**Company:** Hongji Agriculture Co., Ltd.

---

## 1. Food Fraud Vulnerability Assessment Form

The following RPN (Risk Priority Number) matrix evaluates raw material characteristics, supplier control level, substitution complexity, geographical origin, physical form/accessibility, detectability, and risk index for each material in the potato flake supply chain.

| Raw Material | Characteristics / Main Cause | Supplier / Control Level | Substitution Complexity | Geographical Origin | Physical Form / Accessibility | Detectability (D) | Risk Index / Level |
|-------------|------------------------------|--------------------------|------------------------|---------------------|-------------------------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| Potato | Basic agricultural product; excessive impurities (soil, grass, stones) | Level 1 supplier / Easily controlled | Simple substitution (P4) | Ulagai, Hailar, Tongyu, Siziwang Banner, Naiman | Bulk / Very accessible | D2 | R8 / Medium |
| Potato (green skin) | Basic agricultural product; green skin | Easily controlled | Simple substitution (P4) | Naiman | Ton bag / Very accessible | D2 | R8 / Medium |
| Potato (pesticide residues) | Improper pesticide use causing residues | Hard to control | Simple substitution (P5) | Ulagai, Hailar, Tongyu, Siziwang Banner, Naiman | Bulk / Less accessible | D2 | R10 / High |
| Citric acid | Colorless or white crystalline granules, odorless, sour | Level 1 supplier / Hard to control | Easy substitution (P4) | Shandong | Bagged / Accessible | D2 | R8 / Medium |
| Mono- and diglycerides of fatty acids | Milky white to light brown viscous powdery solid; excessive lead | Level 1 supplier / Hard to control | Simple substitution (P4) | Hangzhou | Bagged / Accessible | D2 | R8 / Medium |
| Paper-plastic packaging bag | Food-grade plastic bag; non-food-grade ink | Level 1 supplier / Hard to control | Easy substitution (P4) | Wenzhou | Bagged / Less accessible | D4 | R16 / High |

---

## 2. Risk Level Determination

| Risk Priority Number (RPN = P × D) | Risk Level |
|------------------------------------|-----------|
| 1–4 | Low (L) |
| 5–9 | Medium (M) |
| 10–25 | High (H) |

- **Detectability (D):** D1–D5
- **Probability (P):** P1–P5

---

## 3. High-Risk Materials

The following materials are classified as **High** risk and require enhanced control:

| Material | RPN | Control Measure |
|----------|-----|-----------------|
| Potato (pesticide residues) | R10 | Third-party testing before delivery; supplier reports for 228 pesticide residues (GB 2763) + heavy metals |
| Paper-plastic packaging bag | R16 | Third-party test report per batch for food-grade compliance |
