# Quality & Food Safety Management Manual

**Document No.:** HJ-TD-PE-01-B-2024  
**Supervising Department:** Food Safety Team  
**Company:** Hongji Agriculture Co., Ltd.

---

## 0.1 Company Profile

Hongji Agriculture Co., Ltd. is an agricultural high-tech enterprise engaged in the processing of potato flakes and potato powder, operating an integrated seed-to-flake value chain spanning Hebei and Inner Mongolia.

**Production Lines:** 1 potato flake production line and 4 packaging lines, including destoning/washing machines, steam peelers, post-washing machines, visual inspection machines, blanchers, coolers, cookers, drum dryers, flake breakers, grinders, packaging machines, and metal detectors — supported by raw material and finished product warehouses, a steam supply station, water supply/drainage systems, wastewater treatment, power supply, and heating systems.

**Testing Center:** The company operates an in-house testing center performing sensory inspection and physicochemical analysis (moisture, ash, spots, reducing sugars) and microbiological testing (total plate count, coliforms, molds and yeasts). Type inspection, pathogen testing, and pesticide residue testing are outsourced to accredited third-party laboratories.

**Production:** Seasonal production with shift-based operation. Products are distributed nationwide as food-processing raw materials.

## 1. Scope

The company's Quality & Food Safety Management System is established in accordance with:

- **GB/T 19001:2016 / ISO 9001:2015** — Quality management system requirements
- **FSSC 22000 — Certification Scheme V6**
- **ISO 22000:2018** — Food safety management system requirements
- **ISO/TS 22002-1:2009** — Prerequisite programmes on food safety, Part 1: Food manufacturing

together with applicable laws, regulations, and other requirements.

### Management System Scope

The system covers all departments and processing sites of Hongji Agriculture Co., Ltd. related to the management system, and the product scope covers **the production of potato flakes**.

---

## 2. Management Manual Framework

The management manual is the programmatic document of the company's management system, covering:

1. **Effective planning, implementation, operation, maintenance, updating, and continuous improvement** of the management system.
2. **Control of product quality and food safety hazards** to ensure the consistent supply of safe food for human consumption, while meeting agreed customer requirements and applicable food safety laws and regulations.
3. **Effective communication** with external suppliers, customers, and other relevant parties in the food chain.
4. **Self-evaluation and self-declaration** to ensure continuous improvement of the management system.
